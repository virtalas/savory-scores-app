Japanese Restaurants in Helsinki: How to Find the Authentic Ones

Savory Scores guide · Helsinki · Updated October 2026

Helsinki has more Japanese restaurants than ever, but "Japanese" on the sign can mean anything from a family-run shokudō to an all-you-can-eat sushi buffet. Here's how to tell them apart and find the dish that's actually worth ordering.

The kinds of Japanese restaurants you'll find in Helsinki

Before you pick a place, it helps to know which kind of restaurant you're looking at. Each one does a different job, and the best place for sushi is rarely the best place for katsudon.

  • Sushi bars and omakase counters: small, often reservation-only, built around raw fish and rice. See the best sushi in Helsinki.
  • Ramen shops: one or two broths done well. See the best ramen in Helsinki.
  • Shokudō (everyday diners): rice bowls, curry, tonkatsu and set meals. This is where you'll find katsudon and gyudon.
  • Izakaya: Japanese pubs for small plates, yakitori and drinks. See izakaya in Helsinki.
  • "Asian" restaurants with a Japanese menu: sushi next to pad thai and spring rolls. Some are good, but the menu alone won't tell you.

Why star ratings don't help you choose

Google and Tripadvisor rate the restaurant: the service, the room, the wait. When you're craving Japanese food you usually want to know something narrower: where is the best tempura? A place with 4.6 stars might serve great sushi and a forgettable katsudon.

That's the gap Savory Scores fills. Ratings are attached to dishes, not restaurants, and dishes are grouped into Japanese food categories, so you can compare every gyudon in town side by side.

Authentic vs. "Japanese-style"

Authenticity isn't snobbery. It's useful information: if you grew up in Japan or fell in love with the food there, you want to know which places taste like it. We wrote a whole checklist on how to tell if a Japanese restaurant is authentic. The short version: a short menu, Japanese dish names, rice that's treated with respect, and set meals (teishoku) are all good signs.
