Japanese Grocery Stores in Helsinki: Where to Buy Japanese Food

Savory Scores guide · Helsinki · Updated October 2026

Cooking Japanese at home in Finland is easier than it used to be. Rice, miso, nori and Kewpie mayo are a tram ride away. The harder part is knowing which of the hundred imported products are actually good.

Where to buy Japanese groceries in Helsinki

  • Japanese specialist shops. The most-rated stores in Savory Scores are Tokyokan and HACHI Japanin Markkinat, which focus on Japanese brands: instant noodles, frozen udon, wagashi sweets, real wasabi and harder-to-find items like kombu or katsuobushi.
  • Asian supermarkets. JK Shop (Japanese & Korean), RF Asian Market and East Asia Mart carry Japanese rice, noodles, sauces and snacks alongside Chinese and Korean products.
  • Regular supermarkets. K-Citymarket, Prisma and larger S-Markets have a world-food aisle with sushi rice, soy sauce, nori and wasabi.

A Japanese pantry starter list

  • Short-grain Japanese rice (Japonica)
  • Soy sauce, mirin, rice vinegar, sake for cooking
  • Miso paste and dashi (instant granules or kombu + katsuobushi)
  • Nori, furikake, sesame
  • Japanese curry roux, panko, Kewpie mayonnaise
  • Udon, soba and ramen noodles
