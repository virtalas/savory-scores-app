Best Ramen in Helsinki: How to Find a Great Bowl

Savory Scores guide · Helsinki · Updated October 2026

Helsinki's ramen scene has grown from a couple of noodle bars into a proper choice, which means it's easier than ever to end up with a mediocre bowl. Here's how to judge ramen like a regular and find your favourite fast.

Know your broth first

"Best ramen" depends on what you like. Most Helsinki ramen shops specialise in one or two of these:

StyleBrothOrder it if…
TonkotsuPork bones boiled for hours into a creamy, opaque soupyou want rich and filling
ShoyuClear chicken or pork stock seasoned with soy sauceyou want classic, savoury Tokyo-style ramen
ShioLight, clear broth seasoned with saltyou want to taste the stock itself
MisoBroth blended with fermented soybean pasteit's −15°C outside
Vegan / mushroom dashiKombu and shiitake stock, sometimes with soy milkyou don't eat meat, or want something lighter

What separates a great bowl from an okay one

  • The broth has depth. It should taste of something long-simmered, not of stock powder and salt.
  • Noodles match the soup. Thin, firm noodles for tonkotsu; wavier, thicker ones for miso. If you like them firm, ask for katame.
  • The egg (ajitama) is marinated, with a jammy yolk. It's a quick tell of how much care goes into the kitchen.
  • Chashu is tender and seared, not dry slices from a pack.
  • It arrives fast and hot. Ramen is meant to be eaten immediately. Slurping is allowed.

How to find the best ramen in Helsinki with Savory Scores

  1. Search "Ramen" in the Restaurants tab. You'll see every ramen in Helsinki that people have rated, with photos and average scores.
  2. Switch between All reviews and your own to compare the crowd's favourite with yours.
  3. Rate each bowl you eat by answering one question: Would you have it again? Nope, Maybe, or Yeah.
  4. Fine-tune with the slider. Savory Scores shows your previous ramen scores while you rate, so an 8 always means the same thing to you.
  5. After a few bowls you have a personal ramen top list, so next time you know exactly where to go.
