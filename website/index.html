How it works

Three steps from craving to the best bowl in town.

  1. 1

    Search a Japanese dish

    Type katsudon, ramen, tempura or onigiri. See every version in Helsinki with real photos, the restaurant and its average score, and sort by score or by authenticity.

  2. 2

    Rate it in one tap

    After your meal, answer one question: would you have it again? Fine-tune on a 10-point slider next to your previous scores.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. Each one links to a full guide.

Where can I find authentic Japanese food in Helsinki?

Look past the restaurant's star rating to the individual dishes. Savory Scores lets you search Helsinki's Japanese food by category, such as katsudon, ramen or tempura, and sort every version by score or by authenticity.

Learn more: Japanese restaurants in Helsinki →
Where is the best ramen in Helsinki?

It depends on your broth: tonkotsu, shoyu, shio, miso or vegan. Search "Ramen" in Savory Scores to compare every rated bowl in Helsinki, then build your own ramen top list as you go.

Learn more: the best ramen in Helsinki →
Where can I get good sushi in Helsinki?

Skip the all-salmon buffets and look for places where nigiri and sashimi lead the menu, the rice is body-temperature, and the fish varies. In Savory Scores you can search exact dishes like sushi moriawase or chirashi and see real photos.

Learn more: the best sushi in Helsinki →
How can you tell if a Japanese restaurant is authentic?

A short menu, Japanese dish names, teishoku set meals, proper short-grain rice and cooked dishes beyond sushi are all good signs. Savory Scores also lets diners rate authenticity separately from taste.

Learn more: 8 signs of an authentic Japanese restaurant →
What is katsudon, and where can I eat it in Helsinki?

Katsudon is a breaded pork cutlet simmered with egg and onion in dashi, served over rice. Search "Katsudon" in Savory Scores to see every katsudon in Helsinki ranked by score, with photos.

Learn more: katsudon, gyudon and donburi in Helsinki →
Are there izakayas in Helsinki?

Yes. Several Helsinki restaurants serve izakaya-style small plates like yakitori, karaage and takoyaki with Japanese drinks. Rate each plate in Savory Scores so you remember which ones to order again.

Learn more: izakaya in Helsinki →
Where can I buy Japanese groceries in Helsinki?

Asian supermarkets in central Helsinki and Hakaniemi carry Japanese rice, sauces, noodles and snacks, and larger supermarkets have the basics. Savory Scores' Stores tab lets you rate store-bought items like onigiri and gyoza.

Learn more: Japanese grocery stores in Helsinki →
Where can I have a Japanese lunch in Helsinki?

Many Japanese restaurants in Helsinki serve weekday lunch: teishoku set meals, donburi, ramen and sushi sets, usually cheaper than dinner. Search a dish in Savory Scores to pick today's lunch.

Learn more: Japanese lunch in Helsinki →
Is Savory Scores useful outside Helsinki?

Savory Scores is a catalog of Japanese food in Helsinki, so it's most useful if you live in or are visiting the Helsinki area. Visitors can browse and save dishes before their trip.

Learn more: Japanese food in Finland →
Is Savory Scores free, and how does rating work?

Yes, Savory Scores is free for iPhone and iPad. To rate a dish you answer one question, "Would you have it again?" (Nope, Maybe or Yeah), and can fine-tune it on a 10-point slider next to your previous scores.
