Japanese Food in Finland: A Guide for Visitors & New Residents

Savory Scores guide · Helsinki · Updated October 2026

Finland and Japan have a surprising amount in common: quiet, design, saunas and baths, and a love of good fish. If you're visiting or have just moved here and miss Japanese food, here's what to expect and where to look.

Where the Japanese food is

Most of Finland's Japanese restaurants, sushi counters and Asian grocery stores are in the Helsinki capital region (Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa). Tampere, Turku and Oulu have good options too, but if you want variety, from ramen to izakaya to shokudō rice bowls, Helsinki is where to look. That's also why Savory Scores focuses on Helsinki.

What to expect

  • Sushi is everywhere, including buffets and supermarkets, but quality varies a lot. See the best sushi in Helsinki.
  • Salmon is king. Finnish and Norwegian salmon is excellent, so salmon nigiri is often a highlight.
  • Everyday Japanese food is the hidden gem. Katsudon, curry, teishoku and ramen are increasingly easy to find. See donburi in Helsinki.
  • Lunch is the best deal. Weekday lunch menus are cheaper than dinner. See Japanese lunch in Helsinki.
  • Prices are Nordic. Expect to pay more than in Japan, and more for dinner than lunch.

For Japanese residents and visitors

If you're from Japan, your opinion is exactly what Helsinki's Japanese food scene needs. Savory Scores is available in Japanese, and every dish can be rated for authenticity as well as taste, so others can find the places that taste like home. (Savory Scores は日本語にも対応しています。)

Planning a trip? How to use Savory Scores before you go

  1. Download the app and browse Helsinki's Japanese dishes by category before your trip.
  2. Save the dishes and restaurants you want to try. They'll be waiting in your saved list when you land.
  3. Use Open in Maps to get there.
  4. Rate what you eat, and help the next traveller.

Note: Savory Scores is a catalog of Japanese food in Helsinki. Outside Finland there's nothing to find in it yet.
